My Story So Far

Seven turns that shaped how I work.

2019 — 2025

2019

First heard about UX from Senior from IIT Roorkee working at Google

One conversation put a name to the thing I had been circling around for years.

The spark

2020

Graduated from Architecture

Five years of drawing for people who would live inside the result. That instinct never left.

Foundation

2021

Used Figma & Started Learning UX Design

Self-taught, at odd hours, rebuilding apps I used daily just to understand why they worked.

Learning

2021

First UX Design Job, a Hospitality Startup Bag2Bag at Bangalore

No design system, no research budget. I learned to ship with whatever was actually available.

First job

2024

2nd UX Design Job at Arci-tech, Mumbai leading a team of 6 Designers

Going from making the work to enabling six people to make it changed what I paid attention to.

Leading

2024

Learned Product Management from Upraised

I wanted to argue about priorities in the language the room was already speaking.

Learning

2025

3rd UX Design Job, For a Miami based AI Startup, Remote

Designing for a product whose behaviour is probabilistic. Most of my old certainties needed rewriting.

Present

Now

The next turn hasn’t happened yet.
